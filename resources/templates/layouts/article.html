!New Packaging, Fresh Products: Diamond CBD Just Got a New Look

April 15, 2025 3 minutes read

Diamond CBD is proud to announce an important transition in the way we package and ship many of our most popular products.

As part of our commitment to sustainability, product freshness, and cost-efficiency, we are phasing out our traditional clear plastic jars in favor of resealable, U.S.-made paper bags.

This change affects a wide range of products—including gummies, capsules, flower, pre-rolls, vapes, and more—and reflects our ongoing mission to deliver high-quality goods in a way that benefits both our customers and the planet.

Reducing Waste, Improving Impact

The new resealable paper bags reduce packaging waste by over 70% compared to our previous plastic jars. In addition to minimizing landfill impact, these bags are lighter and more compact, which helps reduce shipping waste and emissions across the supply chain.

This change is part of our broader effort to create a more sustainable operation without compromising on quality or safety.
