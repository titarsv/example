To complete your order
Follow These Steps to Complete Your Order:
  1. Log into your crypto wallet. If you don't have cryptocurrency yet, you can purchase it from any crypto exchange or refer to our manual at https://weedwonderland.co.uk/how-to-pay-with-crypto-currency/ for detailed instructions on how to create one.
  2. Select the "Send" option within your crypto wallet and choose the appropriate wallet from which you'll be sending the funds.
  3. Paste the wallet address provided in your order into the recipient's address field. Ensure that you copy the address accurately to avoid any errors.
  4. Paste the exact amount of cryptocurrency specified in your order. Please note that some providers deduct fees from the amount you intend to send, resulting in the recipient receiving less than the intended amount. Make sure to include any applicable fees in the total amount sent.
  5. Initiate the transfer of funds to our wallet and confirm the transaction. Double-check all details before proceeding to ensure accuracy.
  6. After initiating the transaction, please allow time for it to be confirmed. The duration of confirmation may vary depending on the cryptocurrency and the timing of your transaction, but it could take up to 4 hours in some cases.

Once the transaction is confirmed, your order will be processed accordingly. If you encounter any issues or require further assistance, feel free to reach out to us for support.
